A Mother's Love

A mother is a person, so kind and so dear,
A love that's unconditional and always near.

She nurtures and cares, with a heart so true,
And helps us grow, with a love that's pure and new.

The memories we make with our mothers so sweet,
Are memories that last, and forever repeat.

From bad time stories told to laughter and to tears,
Our mothers are always there, through all our fears.

The lessons we learn from our mothers so wise,
Are lessons that stay and forever surprise.

The support we receive from our mothers so true,
Is support that helps us, to see our dreams come through.

The love we feel from our mothers so real,
Is a love that's unconditional and forever will reveal.

From the moment we're born to the day we grow old,
Our mothers love us with a love that's pure and bold.

From graduating from college to landing a new job,
Our mothers are proud and their joy is something to rob.

The legacy our mothers leave when they pass away,
Is a legacy that's remembered for many a day.

In conclusion our mothers are a treasure so rare,
A blessing that we should always cherish and share.

From the love they give to the lessons they teach,
Our mothers are a gift that's forever to be reached.
